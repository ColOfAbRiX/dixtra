enum MultiBodyPolicy(val key: String)

Where the bodies of a construct with several of them go relative to their separators. A group is never mixed: when the bodies are all on the lines of their separators, or all below them, the policy decides whether they stay; when they are mixed, it decides which way they all go. A group that should be on the lines of its separators goes below them when one body cannot be joined: it does not fit there on one line (once the arrows of the cases are aligned), a comment follows its separator, or it holds an if, a match or a try. A group of one body is never mixed, so both -mixed policies leave it as written.

When select chains break.

Whether a chain in the arguments of a call keeps the line breaks of its author.
